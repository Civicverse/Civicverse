CivicVerse — The Foyer & CivicWatch README
Overview

CivicVerse is a decentralized, non-custodial civic ecosystem that blends real-world work, digital identity, and immersive multiplayer worlds.

Two core systems interoperate seamlessly:

CivicWatch — your real-time civic job and volunteer layer

The Foyer — the immersive multiplayer lobby city where your CivicWatch avatar enters the CivicVerse

Together, they connect real-world contribution with persistent digital identity, allowing actions in one space to carry over into the other.

🏛️ The Foyer — CivicVerse Lobby City

The Foyer is the multiplayer lobby city for CivicVerse. It’s not just a spawn point, but a living, decentralized city that evolves with player actions, real-world missions, and community contributions.

Built in Unreal Engine 5 (Godot prototype also in development)

Persistent, high-fidelity, civilian-owned multiplayer environment

Visual energy comparable to GTA Online, Fortnite, and Roblox

Supports indie devs, educators, and studios to build immersive CivicVerse-compliant worlds

👤 CivicWatch — Real-Time Civic Jobs & Volunteering

CivicWatch is a persistent civic workforce layer integrated with The Foyer.

Real-time job board, volunteer network, and live mission layer

Tracks real-world work, reputation, and skill progression

Location-based gameplay powers avatar movement in The Foyer

Paid or volunteer work, fully verified and auditable

Key Features

Multiple Careers: Work as construction, service, delivery, volunteer, or emergency responder

Live Overworld Map: See active jobs, missions, and other operators

AI + Safety Layer: Training, PPE confirmation, compliance, and fraud detection

Crypto Rewards: Smart contracts release payment, reputation, and skill level-ups

🔄 Interoperability — Foyer & CivicWatch

CivicVerse merges digital and physical contribution through seamless integration:

Feature	CivicWatch (Real-World)	The Foyer (Digital)
| Feature                   | CivicWatch (Real-World)                | The Foyer (Digital)                              |
| ------------------------- | -------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------ |
| Avatar                    | Earned through verified work           | Imported into lobby and connected worlds         |
| Reputation                | Built through jobs, volunteer missions | Visible in social arena, banners, skill badges   |
| Gear & Wearables          | Earned via missions, crypto purchase   | Avatar cosmetics, NFT wearables, real-world sync |
| DAO Weight & Karma        | Skill-based civic contribution         | Persistent across Foyer worlds and portals       |
| Job & Mission Interaction | Live map, dispatch, volunteer work     | Spectate, tip, or join missions from lobby       |

How it works:

Observe from the Foyer: Public displays show active missions, updates, and reputation metrics.

Join from the Foyer: Scan QR or use local device to participate in CivicWatch missions.

Carryover & Rewards: Avatar stats, gear, reputation, and skill progression are synced between real-world CivicWatch activity and The Foyer.

Impact: Real-world actions shape digital identity, and digital reputation unlocks opportunities in immersive worlds.
